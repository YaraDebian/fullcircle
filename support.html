Common questions

How do I save something from another app?

Tap the Share button in Safari, Instagram, or any app, then choose Full Circle. If you don't see it, scroll the row of apps to the end, tap More, and turn Full Circle on.

I'm not getting notifications

Check that notifications are allowed for Full Circle in your iPhone's Settings, then check which kinds are switched on in the app's Settings. If quiet hours are on, nothing arrives between 22:00 and 8:00.

How do I stop the AI from reading my saved content?

In the app, go to Settings and turn off “Read my saved content” to keep your items out of your weekly picks and monthly read. Saving links, “Surprise me”, and the wording of some notifications still use AI; the privacy policy explains exactly what each one sends.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything you saved, including photos. If you can't sign in, email us from the address you signed up with and we'll do it for you.

Can I get a copy of my data?

Yes. Email us from the address you signed up with and we'll send you an export.
