Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026

Full Circle (“the app”) helps you save things you want to do or appreciate, and close the loop on them. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us process it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have.

In short: we collect what you put into the app so the app can work. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites. Some of your content is processed by AI to power features, and you can limit that. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app.

Who we are

Full Circle is developed by Yara Debian, who is the data controller for the app (“we”, “us”). For anything about your privacy, email support@getfullcircle.app.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password. The password is stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider, never in plain text.
  • What you save: titles, notes on why you saved something, links, photos you attach, type, timing preferences, reminders, status, ratings, and reflections.
  • Content from links you save: when you save a link, our server fetches that page to fill in the title and preview image and to write a short summary. The page text and summary are stored with the item.
  • What the app creates for you: your weekly plans and goals, and your monthly reads (a written recap plus counts of what you saved and closed).
  • Settings: whether check-in notifications are on. Display preferences such as theme, haptics and motion, and the AI content setting, stay on your device.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, a push token for your device and its platform (iOS or Android).
  • Technical data: your device's time zone is sent with requests so timing works in your local time; it isn't stored. Our servers process your IP address to deliver requests and limit abuse, and it may appear in short-lived server logs.

We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, precise location, or your contacts.

How AI is used

Full Circle uses Anthropic's Claude API, called from our server, to generate text you see in the app.

  • When you save a link: the link, the page's text or caption, its preview image, and anything you typed are sent to fill in the item and write its summary.
  • Only when “Read my saved content” is on (the default), the titles, notes, types, summaries, ratings, and reflections of your saved items are sent to:
    • choose your weekly and daily picks;
    • pick something when you tap “Surprise me”;
    • word your reminder and resurfacing notifications, if you have them on;
    • write your monthly read.
  • When it's off, none of that content is sent: picks follow simple rules, “Surprise me” picks at random, notifications use plain wording, and your monthly read is based on counts only.

Photos you attach yourself are not sent to the AI. Anthropic processes this data on our behalf only to return a result, doesn't use it to train its models, and keeps it only for a limited period under its commercial terms.

Who processes your data

We use these providers to run the app. Each processes data only on our instructions, under a data processing agreement.

  • Supabase: authentication, database, and photo storage (United States).
  • Render: hosts our server (United States).
  • Anthropic: AI processing, as described above (United States).
  • Expo: app updates and delivery of push notifications. Check-in notifications include the title of the item they're about, so that text passes through Expo and Apple's or Google's push service.

We don't sell or share your personal data with anyone else, except where required by law.

International transfers

Our providers store and process data in the United States. Where data comes from the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, these transfers are protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses in our providers' data processing agreements and, where a provider is certified, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and content: for as long as your account exists.
  • When you delete your account (Settings → Delete account), we permanently delete your account, your saved items, attached photos, plans, monthly reads, settings, and push tokens. Copies in our providers' backups expire on their regular backup schedule.
  • Push tokens are removed when you sign out or when the push service reports they're no longer valid.
  • Server logs are kept for a short period for troubleshooting and security, and are set up to exclude the content of your items.

Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and our providers encrypt stored data at rest. Access controls in the database mean each account can only read and change its own data. Photos are stored privately and shown through links that expire. If a data breach affects your personal data, we will notify the relevant authority and, where required, you, as the law requires.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR), you have the right to:

  • access your data and get a copy of it in a portable format;
  • correct it (you can edit anything you save directly in the app);
  • delete it (in the app, or by emailing us);
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • withdraw consent at any time, for example by turning off notifications or “Read my saved content”;
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

To use any of these rights, email support@getfullcircle.app. We'll reply within one month.

Children

Full Circle is not intended for anyone under 16, and we don't knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above. For significant changes, we'll also let you know in the app.
