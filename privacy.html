Full Circle — Privacy Policy

Effective 25 August 2026

Full Circle (“the app”) is a personal tool for saving things you want to do or appreciate, and closing the loop on them. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Full Circle is developed by Yara Debian (“we”, “us”). For any privacy question, contact us at yaradebian1@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account information — your email address and password, to create and secure your account. Authentication is handled by Supabase; your password is stored only as a secure hash, never in plain text.
  • Your content — the items you save: titles, notes, links, attached photos, item type, reminder timing, ratings, and reflections. This is the core data that makes the app work.
  • Derived activity data — simple aggregates of your saves (counts by type, follow-through timing) used to generate your taste profile.

We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, precise location, or your contacts, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

How your content is used with AI

To generate your taste profile and to help file saved links (auto title, thumbnail, and short summary), the app sends relevant content — item titles, your notes, link summaries, and ratings — to our backend, which calls Anthropic’s Claude API to produce the text you see.

Your control: you can turn off sending your saved content for taste insights with the “read my saved content” toggle in the app.

This data is sent only to produce features you asked for. It is not used to train AI models — Anthropic does not train on data submitted through its API.

Where your data is stored, and who processes it

  • Supabase — authentication, the database that holds your items, and storage for your photos.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — processes content to generate insights and summaries, as described above.
  • Render — hosts our backend API.
  • Expo / EAS — delivers app updates and supports notifications.

These providers process data on our behalf so the app can function.

Notifications

The app schedules local notifications on your device to remind you about things you saved. You can disable them at any time in your device settings.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Settings → delete account); this permanently removes your account and all of the items you saved.

Security

Your data is protected by authentication and per-user row-level security, so only you can read or change your items.

Children

Full Circle is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.
